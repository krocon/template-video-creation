Hey nerds! Does your form have more subscriptions than input fields?
Time for Signal Forms. Since Angular 22, they're stable and production-ready.
Reactive Forms often mean nesting FormGroups, subscribing to valueChanges and fixing types by hand. Lots of boilerplate, and every subscription is a source of bugs.
First: Model first. Your data lives in a plain signal, and the form function wraps your form around it.
Second: Bind with formField. One attribute on the input, and value and state stay in sync, both ways.
Third: Validation in the schema. Add required, email or min right there, with your own error message.
Fourth: Everything is a signal. Read valid, touched and errors straight in the template, no subscribe needed.
So what now? Pick your next new form, import from angular forms signals and start with a signal model. Then migrate existing Reactive Forms step by step.
Remember: Signal first, the form follows!
